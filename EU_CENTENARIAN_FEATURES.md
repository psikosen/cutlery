# EU + Centenarian Feature Backlog

## Why this pack
These ideas align nutrition gameplay with modern EU/WHO patterns and centenarian diet traits:
- frequent legumes/pulses
- high plant diversity and fiber
- lower salt/free sugar exposure
- social/shared meal rituals
- simple, repeatable home-cooking systems

## High impact features
1. Longevity Nutrition Score (daily)
- Score pillars: legumes, plant diversity, fiber, fruit/veg, fish frequency, sodium control, sugar control, hydration.
- UI: mini radar next to domain power chart.
- Hook: score grants small gene drop chance bonus.

2. Weekly Pulse Tracker
- Dedicated tracker for weekly legume servings.
- Goal bands: 2, 4, 6 servings/week.
- Rewards: health and finance synergy bonuses.

3. Plate Composer
- Drag-and-drop meal builder (veg, legumes, whole grains, protein, fats).
- Shows "balance" feedback and unlocks recipe quests.

4. Centenarian Ritual Streaks
- Streaks for "slow meal", "stop at comfortable fullness", "shared meal".
- Separate from task streak to avoid punishing missed workouts.

5. Social Meal Chain
- Lightweight group mechanic: complete one shared healthy meal challenge with a friend/family member.
- Rewards social genes and a weekly bonus chest.

## Medium effort features
6. Grocery Budget Planner
- Enter budget, auto-suggest affordable staple list (beans/lentils/oats/veg/frozen fish).
- Track savings from protein swaps.

7. Pantry Inventory + Waste Guard
- Mark pantry staples and leftovers.
- Quests pull from available ingredients first.

8. Recipe Mutation Lab
- Convert one base recipe into variants (higher fiber, lower sodium, higher protein).
- Successful variants unlock visual creature mutations.

9. Circadian Meal Timing Mode
- Optional mode tracking late-night eating and first meal timing.
- Links to discipline domain bonuses.

10. Label Scanner Mini-game
- Fast compare game for sodium/sugar/fiber labels.
- Trains "mind" domain while supporting finance decisions.

## Fast wins
11. Domain-specific nutrition tips on quest cards
- 1-line practical tip per nutrition quest.

12. Weekly "Centenarian Challenge" rotating quest
- e.g., legumes 4x week, 10-plant day, 20-minute shared meal.

13. Smart quest bundles
- Auto-bundle 3 related quests into one mini-campaign: prep + cook + share.

14. Salt/Sugar alert badges
- Simple red/yellow/green badges based on manual input.

15. Seasonal produce quests
- Rotating quests tied to affordable seasonal vegetables/legumes.

## Suggested build order

2. Weekly Pulse Tracker
3. Centenarian Ritual Streaks
4. Grocery Budget Planner
